# Future directions

The entries below are possibilities only. They are non-committed, are not part
of the current architecture, are not implementation requirements, and are
subject to separate validation, specification, prioritization, and approval.
This is not a dated roadmap and does not promise versions or delivery dates.
The [product direction](product-direction.md) and
[MVP architecture](architecture.md) remain the current sources of authority.

## Possible areas for later validation

These themes may be considered independently in future work, without implying
that any particular design or scope has been selected:

- richer followed-person management;
- authentication and access to private activity;
- repository and organization tracking;
- startup, scheduled, or real-time synchronization, including webhooks;
- mechanisms for retaining or retrieving more historical activity;
- cross-device locking, a command-line client, or additional clients.

Any future work in these areas must receive its own approved contract before
implementation. It must also be checked against local-first operation, privacy,
compatibility, provider limits, note safety, and the detailed specifications
that remain authoritative for the current product.

## Explicit non-goals pending product-direction decisions

The following are product-direction exclusions, not endorsed roadmap items:

- AI summaries or judgment, behavioral analysis, productivity conclusions,
  scoring, or ranking;
- recruitment or employee monitoring;
- lead generation, contact enrichment, or automated outreach;
- bulk people discovery;
- monetization;
- a hosted backend or database;
- telemetry or analytics;
- automated publication.

Reconsidering any of these exclusions requires a future product-direction
decision and a separately approved contract. Until then, they are not product
requirements or implementation work.
